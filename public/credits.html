Sound recordings / Tonaufnahmen

The sounds in this study are 30-second excerpts of recordings from the International Soundscape Database (ISD v1.0; Mitchell, Oberman, Aletta, Erfanian, Kachlicka, Lionello, Kang et al.), doi:10.5281/zenodo.10672568, published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. They were shortened, faded in and out and level-adjusted.
