Sound recordings / Tonaufnahmen

Most sounds in this study are 15-second excerpts of recordings from the International Soundscape Database (ISD v1.0; Mitchell, Oberman, Aletta, Erfanian, Kachlicka, Lionello, Kang et al.), doi:10.5281/zenodo.10672568, published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Some sounds are excerpts of recordings from Silenzi in Quota (Oberman, Latini, Aletta, Gozzi, Kang, Torresin; Sci Rep 15, 28759, 2025, doi:10.1038/s41598-025-08524-y; dataset zenodo.org/records/20719737), recordings provided by the authors. They were shortened, faded in and out and level-adjusted.

Sie wurden gekürzt, ein- und ausgeblendet und im Pegel angepasst.
